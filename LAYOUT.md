# Website and workspace layout

The public website keeps the reference's black/crimson/warm-white palette, Bebas Neue / Inter / Instrument Serif typography, moving portrait hero, grain, particles and smooth reveal effects. Its opening title now reveals Maharashtra Vision Media, expands into the public page, waits briefly for fonts/portraits and can be skipped. Login and Book remain prominent; Replay intro is in the footer.

Public navigation stays short: Services, Work, Studio, Login, Book. Packages, real portfolio/Reel entries, managed handles, testimonials, FAQs and contact details appear only when published through the admin CMS. Instagram reels load on demand. No invented activity or client list appears.

The light client portal has six clear destinations: Your space, Book / Enquire, My shoots, Bills & receipts, Social media, My details. The booking flow shows service cards, inclusions/add-ons, occasion/Other, date, three slots, eligible location, and the applicable policy. Unverified/outside-area venues route to a call/enquiry. There is no fake paid checkout.

Admin has detailed, permission-aware modules: Overview, Orders, Calendar, Packages, Clients/Excel, WhatsApp, Payments/refunds, Bills/receipts, Social management/content approvals, Website CMS, Team/access, Settings/activity. Shared records drive all modules. On the go is a compact route for own assignments, claimable work, fast progress/delivery actions and custom bookings.

See STUDIO_SETUP.md for the backend lifecycle, configuration, provider connection requirements, persistence and verification.
